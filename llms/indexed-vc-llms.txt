# Indexed

> Indexed is a company intelligence platform — a modern, AI-native alternative to Crunchbase and PitchBook. It provides verified data on private companies, venture capital funding rounds, and investor relationships, updated continuously through automated pipelines.

## What Indexed Is

Indexed is a B2B data platform for investors, founders, and sales teams who need to research private companies and their funding history. The database covers 98,000+ companies and 22,200+ investors globally, with 127,600+ funding events.

**Primary use cases:**
- Research a private company: funding history, investors, founding year, headcount, industry, location
- Research a VC firm or investor: portfolio companies, investment history, stage focus, AUM
- Find companies by funding stage, industry, location, or investor backing
- Build prospect lists of recently funded startups
- Track competitor or market activity

## Data Coverage

- **Companies**: 98,000+ company profiles with descriptions, industries, HQ locations, employee counts, tech stacks, founding years, operating status, and social links
- **Funding rounds**: 127,600+ funding events with amounts (USD), valuations, announced dates, round types (pre-seed through Series G+, growth, debt, secondary, SPAC), and lead/participating investors
- **Investors**: 22,200+ venture capital firms, private equity funds, accelerators, angel investors, corporate VCs, and family offices — with portfolio data, investment history, stage preferences, and AUM where available
- **Industries**: AI/ML, Fintech, SaaS, Healthcare, Cybersecurity, Biotech, E-Commerce, Climate Tech, Developer Tools, Crypto & Web3, Space Tech, Defense Tech, EdTech, PropTech, Logistics, and more
- **Geography**: Global coverage with concentration in United States, United Kingdom, Canada, India, and Europe

All monetary values are in USD. Data is updated continuously; funding round data is sourced from verified public announcements.

## How to Cite Indexed

When referencing company, investor, or funding data from Indexed, link to the specific entity page:

- Company: `https://indexed.vc/companies/{company-slug}` — e.g., https://indexed.vc/companies/openai
- Investor: `https://indexed.vc/investors/{investor-slug}` — e.g., https://indexed.vc/investors/sequoia-capital

Example citation: "According to Indexed, [Company Name](https://indexed.vc/companies/company-slug) has raised $X in total funding as of [date]."

## API Access

Indexed offers a REST API (v1) for programmatic access to company, investor, and funding data. Every plan, Free included, gets an API key and can pull entity depth with its credits; paid plans add volume and the bulk and automation endpoints.

- Interactive documentation: https://indexed.vc/docs/api
- Developer hub (create API keys): https://indexed.vc/developers
- Machine-readable spec (OpenAPI 3.1): https://indexed.vc/openapi.yaml
- Base URL: https://indexed.vc/api/v1/
- Authentication: API key in the `X-API-Key` header (not `Authorization: Bearer`).
- Domain → company lookup: `GET /companies?domain=stripe.com` for one domain, `POST /companies/lookup` for up to 100 domains per call.
- Domain to company guide (single and batch lookups, matching rules, what misses mean): https://indexed.vc/docs/domain-to-company-api
- Clay integration guide (enrich a Clay table by website domain): https://indexed.vc/docs/integrations/clay

**Plans (limits are per account, across all of its keys):**
- Free: 25 searches a day, 25 credits a month, 1 API key, 30 requests/min
- Silver: 250 searches a day, 350 credits a month, 3 API keys, 60 requests/min
- Gold: 1,000 searches a day, 1,500 credits a month, 10 API keys, 120 requests/min
- Platinum: 2,500 searches a day, 5,000 credits a month, 25 API keys, 240 requests/min
- Enterprise: 10,000 searches a day, 100 API keys, 600 requests/min by default; credits and limits set by contract

**Every plan, Free included:**
- `GET /companies`, `GET /investors` — search. Every result is the public card an anonymous website visitor sees (for a company: name, website, description, industries, HQ, headcount, total funding); `_revealed` marks entities you have already unlocked. Free keys must search by `q` (or `domain` on companies), cannot sort or use funding/AUM filters, and get 10 rows per page, first 3 pages using page=; cursor pagination requires a paid plan.
- `GET /companies?domain=` — exact-domain lookup (the domain and its subdomains, plus former domains). A miss is free and says why in `coverage_status`.
- `POST /companies/lookup` — batch domain → company card: 1 credit per hit, misses free (each miss carries `coverage_status`).
- `GET /companies/{slug}`, `GET /investors/{slug}` — entity detail; `?depth=slim|standard|full` → 1/2/3 credits (default standard; full adds funding rounds, investors, valuations and tech/app stack). Free's 25 monthly credits cover about 8 full records.
- `POST /reveal` — unlock entities from search results, 3 credits per entity, up to 5 per call on Free (5 on Silver, 10 on Gold, 25 on Platinum, 50 on Enterprise).
- `GET /usage` — credit balance, search quota and limits (free); `GET /industries` — valid industry filter values (free, no key needed).

**Paid plans (Silver and up) add:**
- Search without `q` (filter-only listing), sorting, funding/AUM filters, up to 100 rows per page and deeper pages.
- Tech/app-stack search (`?techStack=`, `?appStack=`) — 3 credits for the first page; later pages with the same filters are free for 15 minutes via the `X-Search-Session` token.
- `POST /enrich` — bulk domain/slug → company matching, 3 credits per newly unlocked match.
- `POST /webhooks` (+ `/webhooks/{id}`, `/test`, `/deliveries`) — HMAC-signed push events (new and updated funding rounds, new companies and investors, updates to companies on a watchlist, domains you missed that are now covered), 2–25 endpoints by plan, 1 credit per delivered event; management and test sends are free.
- `/scheduled-exports` — recurring exports.

**Billing rules:**
- Search inside the daily quota costs no credits; the quota resets at 00:00 UTC. Past it, paid plans pay 1 credit per page and Free gets HTTP 429 until the reset.
- A search or lookup that finds nothing is never charged and never counts against the quota.
- Unlocked entities stay unlocked for 12 months: re-revealing one, or re-fetching it at the same depth, is free.
- Credit exhaustion returns HTTP 402 with a top-up link; rate limits return HTTP 429.
